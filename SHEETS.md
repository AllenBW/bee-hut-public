## 28. Corners, hold-downs and anchors

![Corners, hold-downs and anchors](png/28-corners_holdowns.png)

How the hut is held together and held down. The front-left corner in plan, at three times the scale: the side wall runs through with a two-stud corner, the front wall's end stud stands inside it, and a nailer stud in the side wall catches the side wall's T&G where it meets the front wall's; the front wall's cap plate runs 3-1/2 past its end over the side wall's top plate, so the plates, the pyramid's tension ring, lap at every corner. The hut is braced as three walls working through the roof, every wall sheathed above and below its openings (continuous sheathing): each side wall's back corner panel and the front wall either side of the door, nailed 8d at 4 in on the edges and 12 in in the field, with a strap hold-down rated 1400 lb at each of the eight posts marked HD on sheet 21 and sheet 23. Each strap runs down the post, through a slot in the deck, and wraps the rim or the block under it: at the corners on down the skid's face, at the door kings round the front rim's three plies. The side walls' front ends, cut by the intakes, and the back wall, all openings, brace nothing. The hut stands on six helical anchors driven vertically beside the edge skids, 4-1/4 out from each skid's face so they clear its blocks, 6 in from each end and one at the middle between two joists: driven on the layout in October before the blocks go in, then each bracketed with an offset bracket and through-bolted to its skid (and the outrigger where it laps), and one of them pull-tested to 1000 lb uplift and 300 lb sideways in this soil. Three braced walls working through the roof is not the IRC's prescriptive bracing, so whatever the county's classification, an engineer checks the wind and anchorage on one page.

## 34. Control board

![Control board](png/34-control_board.png)

The year-one control, on the left front module: Sharashkin's slotted planks in place of the double screen, so one colony shows what the double screen costs or saves.
