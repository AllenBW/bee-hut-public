## 17. Hatch cartridge

![Hatch cartridge](png/17-hatch_cartridge.png)

Section A-A at the wall, large scale. The cartridge is a 1/2 in plywood sleeve the depth of the wall (5-1/2), with the cedar trim frame outside (3/4 proud of the siding), the aluminium pan on its sill falling outward, brush seals at the room end filling the 1/4 in clearance, and the gravity flap: a 1/2 in plywood leaf on a piano hinge under the bench ledger. It slides into a bare 30-1/2 × 14-1/8 rough opening from outside and is screwed through the jambs; the sill sits 3 above the deck so the sleeve sill top is flush with the rail tops. There are no interior stops: the module pushes the flap up and it lies on the board; when the module leaves, the flap drops to the runner and the room stays bee-tight. Build eight: seven to fit and one kept as the gauge for framing the openings.
