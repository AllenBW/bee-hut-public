# Bee hut

A 10 × 10 ft pine-framed hut on pressure-treated skids, cedar where a hand or a foot touches it, with horseshoe benches over seven identical hive modules on Langstroth deep frames. Every module rolls out through its wall onto an apron and opens from inside. A double screen board is the only thing between a guest and the bees, and the bench above it is half open air.
